# ARML 1.0: Arabi-Malayalam legacy encoding

A single-byte, pre-shaped, visual-order encoding for software that cannot shape or
reorder Arabic script (old DTP tools, video titlers, some embedded systems). It is
the Arabi-Malayalam counterpart of FML. Reference code: `src/lib/arml/`.

## What an ARML string is

- Ordinary Windows-1252 text. Each glyph has one code; an ARML font draws it.
- **Pre-shaped:** every letter is already in its isolated, initial, medial or final form.
- **Visual order:** each line is stored left to right as it is drawn. Runs of Arabic are
  reversed; numbers and Latin keep their own order.
- **Marks** (vowels, shadda, sukun...) are zero-advance glyphs placed straight after the
  letter they sit on. Shadda with fatha, damma, Arabi-Malayalam e or o is one stacked glyph.
- Lines are independent. A program that wraps long lines will wrap them in the wrong place;
  keep one line per text box, or break lines yourself.

## Codes

- 189 glyphs, listed in `glyph-table.json` (generated by `scripts/export-arml-table.mjs`).
- Space, digits 0-9 and `! " ' ( ) , - . / : ; ?` stay as themselves. `(` and `)` are
  swapped in Arabic runs so they draw the right way round.
- Every other ASCII symbol, the printable 0x80-0x9F slots and 0xA1-0xFF (not 0xAD) hold glyphs.
- Latin letters therefore collide with glyph codes. Mixed text needs a Latin font on the
  Latin runs, exactly as with FML. The converter keeps Latin letters as typed and reports them.
- 4 codes are spare for future glyphs.

## Stability

**Never reorder or change an existing glyph.** Fonts bind to these codes. New glyphs are
appended to `GLYPH_SPECS` in `glyphs.ts`; a test freezes the first entries.

## What a font must provide

The 189 glyphs under the names in `glyph-table.json`, with Arabic joining already
resolved: the `.init`, `.medi` and `.fina` shapes must connect cleanly. Marks need
mark-to-base positioning baked in or anchored through GPOS. There are no lam-alef
ligatures: lam is drawn in its joining shape and alef in its final shape.

## Not covered

Latin text, Malayalam script, Tatweel, Quranic annotation marks, and lam-alef
ligatures. The converter lists the characters it leaves out.
